What is Gonnet?

Gonnet is a QR-based digital identity platform that enables users to create secure digital profiles for vehicles, personal belongings, pets, and emergency contacts. By scanning a Gonnet QR code, users can access the information intentionally shared by the owner and connect quickly when needed.

How It Works
    • Purchase and activate your unique Gonnet QR sticker.
    • Create your secure digital profile.
    • Choose what information you wish to display.
    • Attach the QR sticker to your vehicle or belongings.
    • Anyone scanning the QR code can instantly access your shared profile information.
What You Can Add
  • Name, mobile, email
  • Emergency contact details
  • Social links & personal info
  • Vehicle or other details
Privacy Control
  • You control what is visible
  • You decide which information is visible on your public profile.
  • You can modify or update your profile at any time.
  • Your information is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy.
Access & Edit
  • Login using your email
  • Edit via OTP verification
  • Access anytime via your QR link
Important Notes
  • Each mobile number can be used only once
  • Same email can have multiple profiles
  • You can update your profile anytime
